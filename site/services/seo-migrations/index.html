How I protect search visibility during a migration

Know which URLs matter before moving

I inventory the existing pages and group them by what should be kept, consolidated or retired. Where available, I review analytics and Search Console to identify pages bringing in search traffic or receiving links. We map old URLs to their closest relevant new destinations before the Webflow build goes live.

Check the launch from both sides

After launch, I test representative 301 redirects, internal links, canonical URLs, metadata and sitemap entries. I also check that important pages can be accessed and indexed. If you have Search Console access, we can use it to monitor indexing and broken links after the move.

Frequently asked questions

Should I keep the existing domain?
Often it makes sense to keep your domain during a platform change. If you also need to change domains, we scope the redirect mapping and launch checks for both.
Does a migration guarantee my rankings will stay the same?
Search positions can fluctuate after a move. The work reduces avoidable problems such as broken URLs, long redirect chains and missing page metadata.
What if the site has hundreds of URLs?
Share a sitemap, CMS export or current site link. The volume and complexity of the pages help determine how the URL map and launch checks are handled.
