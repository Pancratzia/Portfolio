What goes into a Figma-to-Webflow build

Build for real content and devices

I translate your designs into Webflow components, responsive layouts and, when needed, CMS collections. We agree how layouts should adapt to smaller screens and which text, images and entries your team needs to edit. I test with representative content so the build handles longer titles, multiple entries and realistic image sizes.

Connect forms and prepare handoff

I review form destinations, key interactions, internal links and page titles before handoff. We agree who owns the Webflow account and what your team will update. If a feature needs a third-party service or custom JavaScript, I identify it during scoping so the implementation and ownership are clear.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build a website from an existing Figma file?
Yes. Send the design link and page inventory. I’ll review components, breakpoints, CMS requirements and interactions before quoting.
Will my team be able to edit it?
We agree which content belongs in the CMS or editable sections, and I review how to update it at handoff.
Can you work on a site already built in Webflow?
Yes. An existing site can be scoped as a focused feature, a new set of pages or a rebuild, depending on how the current project is organized.
