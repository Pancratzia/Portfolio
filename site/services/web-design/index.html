How the website design takes shape

Start with what visitors need to do

I review your offer, audience and existing content before designing screens. A service business may need separate pages for its core offers, while a campaign may be better served by a single landing page. We decide what pages deserve their own layout and what action each page should lead to.

Approve a direction in Figma

The homepage establishes the visual direction. Once it’s approved, I extend the system through typography, colors, reusable components and responsive layouts. The handoff includes the screens and patterns your development team needs to build the site consistently.

Frequently asked questions

Can I hire you just for website design?
Yes. I can deliver a Figma handoff for your team or carry the approved design into Webflow.
What should I bring to the project?
Share your current site if you have one, existing brand assets, service information and any page copy you already have. We identify content gaps while planning the layouts.
Is this a fit for an existing website?
Yes, if the layout, messaging or visual hierarchy needs attention. If the underlying site also needs to change, a website rebuild can cover both design and implementation.
