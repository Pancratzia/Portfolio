When it makes sense to rebuild a Webflow website

Separate the parts worth keeping from the problems

I review the published site and its underlying project to see where issues live. Sometimes the visual design needs a refresh, but the CMS and URLs are solid. In other cases, repeated sections, broken responsive layouts and hard-to-edit templates justify rebuilding the structure around the content.

Plan the switch before editing the live site

I document which pages, components and integrations will change, what content carries over and how we test the replacement. If URLs change, the launch plan includes relevant redirects. For a duplication project, we agree how the two versions differ by audience, domain, branding and content ownership.

Frequently asked questions

Can a redesign keep the same URLs?
Yes. We can keep existing URLs where they still serve the same purpose and plan redirects for pages that need to move.
Can we rebuild only part of the website?
Yes. A homepage, service section or CMS template can be scoped separately if the rest of the site is working well.
Can you duplicate a Webflow site for another brand?
Yes, if you have the rights and account access needed to reuse it. We scope the content, design and integration changes for the new version.
