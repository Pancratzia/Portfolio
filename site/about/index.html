About

Hi, I’m Laura Ortega.

I’m a Webflow Certified Partner, designer and developer based in Barquisimeto, Venezuela.

Independent Webflow partner

Design with an engineering background.

I design and build websites for direct clients and agencies. My work ranges from bilingual law firm sites to searchable directories and platform migrations.

I’ve led a web department and managed website projects. I set the scope, coordinate timelines and feedback, and stay hands-on with design and development through launch.

I studied telematics engineering, which shapes how I approach design. I think about who needs to edit the site, how visitors find information and what will happen to existing URLs during a migration.

I work in English and Spanish and partner remotely with teams around the world.

Outside of work, I have a soft spot for poodles and fantasy worlds.
